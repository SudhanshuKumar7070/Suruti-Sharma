Depression Due to
Change in Profession

calendar_today June 9, 2026 · schedule 4 min read

Let's come together to count the depressed people. If we come to do so then we find that the numbers of depressed people are uncountable and to write about them is likely to spread the ink over the pages.
Depression has become a major problem these days. Depression could be defined as a collective term which represents the group of anxiety, loss of confidence and tension. Today we are going to talk about an issue that works as the pillar for the roof of depression:

CHANGE IN PROFESSION

I know that this problem seems very familiar to us. Today, we are very busy in our lives that we don't have time even to realise that we are depressed.

Kindly think for once if you are happy with the profession that you are having now. I am sure that there would be 99% negative answers. Everyone works so hard since his childhood to make his death ceremony luxurious. It means that we spend our childhood and adulthood just for persuing studies in the field which can give us a better future and a better profession; but today, we have a very tough competition that one gets depressed when he doesn't get the right professional life that he was looking for. This resulted asthe society doesn't let you live peacefully and you get involved in any undesirable profession just for your monetary needs and to shut the mouth of the people living in the society. The seed of 'change in profession' begins to grow from this stage. This seed can take a structure of a poisonous tree where the leaves of stress, blossoms of anxiety , and the fruit of problems grow; and this tree is renowned as the tree of depression among the kind people of this world.

CHANGE IN PROFESSION - A COMMON PROBLEM

Change in profession is a common problem in everyone's life because people are very unstable in nature. If we are not getting good salary then we go for a better option where we can get a better salary but we don't see that we have sold out our relief just to earn some more coins. We all know that everything has its cons and pros; it might be possible that a place where you are getting a good salary, there you might don't equal rights, releif and comfort which you had earlier. Everyone is very hungry for a placid place.

We have heard it several times that 'Health is wealth'. But we take it only as a phrase and don't apply it in our real lives. In reality, our health is everything. If one is physically hurted then the entire world comes to show the sympathy, but if one is mentally hurted and depressed then people don't even take it as a problem and neglect it. Depression can be a cause of the mental disorder which is a major problem.

COVID-19 LED CHANGE IN PROFESSION

Covid-19 destroyed everything and still it is a major problem. Don't worry, this article is about ' Depression due to change in profession ' only, I was just trying to discuss this problem in different different phases and situations. The time of Corona pandemic is the most bad phase ever where the problems of change in profession were found mostly. Why do we accept the way of any profession? I give the answer. We choose any profession due to two reasons :

  • A. To fulfill our needs by earning money from the profession.
  • B. We do have interest about any particular profession and we are passionate about that.

I hope that my answer could make you satisfied . The reasons written here are very easy to understand. Both the reasons are very genuine and relatable but we only follow the reason A. Now I tell you the reason behind my reason, we always go with the first one because our depressed and annoyed mind only wants to run behind the money. The instability of humans' wants and earning expectations cause the problem of change in profession that comes as a problem of depression.

We always think that money can buy our happiness. I won't say that it is completely false but it is also not completely true; it depends on one's attitude , dedication and interest regarding his job . If we are doing our profession with interest, then it would be beneficial for us in every aspect.

CAN'T WE GET A RID OF THIS PROBLEM?

We should make our profession our strength rather than making it a reason for depression. We can get a rid of this problem by accepting following tips:

  1. We should pinpoint the the problem related to our profession that is being a cause for depression in our lives. If we do so, then we can solve the problem on the spot and we don't have to change our profession at that moment, and we change then it will reduce the possibility of being faced the same problem in future.
  2. Always choose the right profession. Here, right profession means the profession in which you are completely interested and bounded and bonded with your heart.
  3. Never choose a illegal profession that makes you worried and pushes you choose other professional life.
  4. You can bring a change in your perspective regarding your career.

I hope , these tips would help you in your lives. I want to suggest you all to save some hours and moments from your valuable time to think and realise that you are depressed and you have work on that. Depression is a problem that could be succeeded in a condition that you are willing to succeed.

Now, I want some time in my fist to think about another topic for my next blog. Finally I want to conclude by saying some lines :

psychology

Pinpoint and face the problems before changing your profession;
Choose the profession where you are interested,
neglect the profession that leads depression;
Say NO to the mental issues
Let's make a physically and mentally fit nation.
