Decode the box

edit Poetry

Let’s unbox the box made
With circuits and lights;
An ocean of languages exists
But it chose to talk in bits and bytes;
Do you know? Algorithms are set
To run it, it owes internet
Too with World Wide Web
Is like the deep grave
In this depressed world to be dug
To solve the riddles through 'Debug'.

Programming languages like C, C++ and Java
Have been explored by this box,
Now this Terra is ready to unbox
Other languages, the rookies
Like Kotlin and PHP to be
Served with sweet web cookies.

Now we are moving towards Artificial Intelligence,
Are we going be dependent on it?
Are we going to see this world through the google lense?
Is it going to snatch my job?
Are we employees going to sob?
We humans created AI , this input
And output devices , this RAM and ROM;
So it will be like to hole in the plate you ate from.

Laugh Out Loud! Going to inform the crowd ;
Don’t worry! Don’t be hurt!
We are these computer’s heart.
If we won’t work, Perhahps
You don’t know , these computers will collapse.
Going to reveal , going to unbox
The fact that we created this rather
than been created by this box .

Broken

edit Poetry

Once the bleeding-heart's heart says,
"Oh sweet soil, you must feel proud,
Even after knowing that you are weak,
We never neglect you in our clique."
" I was the strongest. Don't satirize",Soil shouts.
The blossoms laugh, and say,
"Oh poor soil, really? We have many doubts."
"I could also enjoy the grace of cloud;
I wish, this heart had never fallen in love,
Then ,You too would have felt proud."

"Oh broken soil, tell us your story,
We're all set to know, How did you loose your glory?"
"I was very high, even more than your dream;
In front of me, the waves of my darling
sea always used to gleam.
My heart got melted as
the snow was getting melted on me;
I couldn't think anything more,
I got trapped in the love of my darling sea.

I couldn't meet my love as it touched my feet;
I was at the top;
for the millions of years , we couldn't meet.
I got broken in to the rocks ,
Now, I was excited to finally meet her;
The day when I would fall in her arm,
With my innocence, I would greet her.
But I sank, when I fell;
Noone could even see my tears,
About that , I can't even tell.

Now my darling was at the top,
I was sleeping in the Earth's lap;
I decided to be broken in to the soil,
Actually we get lost when we are in love's trap.
Finally , I got mixed with her;
Once the wind blew up to stir.
Finally, my hand was in her hand;
But atlast , I got betrayed as every inmorato,
After a long journey, she left me
alone in a strange land.
I want to go to the wheel of trust's hub;
And want to ask again and again,
Why did I fall in love?"

Breeding-heart smiles and says,
"After listening this story,I have awoken;
Today, I came to know that
basically you are broken."
